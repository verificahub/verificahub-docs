# Privacy policy

> **Draft — pending legal review.** This document is a draft for review and is not yet in effect.

## 1. Who processes personal data

1.1. **Aigiz Iskuzhin, sole proprietor**, operates Verificahub (the “Provider”).

- Website: https://verificahub.ru
- Dashboard: https://app.verificahub.ru
- Privacy contact: privacy@verificahub.ru, +7 927 938-35-62

1.2. This policy describes how personal data is processed and protected when using Verificahub.

## 2. The Provider's two roles

2.1. **As a controller**, the Provider processes the personal data of people who register for and use the platform (the “User” or “Customer”).

2.2. **As a processor**, the Provider processes end users' phone numbers submitted by the Customer for verification. The Customer is the controller of that data; the Provider acts on the Customer's instructions and on their behalf.

## 3. Data we process

3.1. **User / Customer data:** first name, last name, email address and phone number.

3.2. **End user data processed on the Customer's instructions:** phone numbers submitted for verification and technical request data, including the time, method and verification result.

3.3. **Technical data:** IP address, device and browser information, and cookies to the extent needed to operate and secure the Service.

## 4. Purposes of processing

4.1. Registration and platform access, verification services, billing, technical support, communication with the User and Service security.

## 5. Grounds for processing

5.1. Processing is based on the data subject's consent, the contract with the data subject ([Public offer](./public_offer.md)), the Customer's instructions to process end user data, and applicable legal requirements.

## 6. Retention and protection

6.1. The Provider takes organizational and technical measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, destruction and other unlawful actions.

6.2. Data is retained until the purposes of processing have been achieved or consent is withdrawn, unless applicable law requires otherwise.

## 7. Sharing with third parties

7.1. The Provider may use telecommunications suppliers to deliver Services such as SMS and calls. Data is shared only to the extent necessary to perform the contract and in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

## 8. Data subject rights and requests

8.1. Data subjects may request information about the processing of their data, request its correction, restriction or deletion, and withdraw consent.

8.2. Send privacy requests or withdrawal of consent to privacy@verificahub.ru, or call +7 927 938-35-62. The Provider handles requests within the time limits required by applicable law.

See also [Personal data consent](./personal_data_consent.md).
